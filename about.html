About KalingaPH

A student project about mental health support for Filipino learners.

Theme: SDG 4, Quality Education

Students learn best when they feel safe and supported. In the Philippines, bullying, stress, and limited school counselors can get in the way of learning. KalingaPH (kalinga means “care”) helps students, teachers, and families find support. It links to UN SDG 4 (opens in a new tab).

Objectives

  • Make trusted help easy to find in a few clicks.
  • Give plain-language guidance to students, teachers, and parents.
  • Be usable by people with different abilities, devices, and connections.

Who it is for

Basic education and college students, teachers and guidance staff, and parents or guardians. Many users are on phones and slow connections, so the pages are light.
