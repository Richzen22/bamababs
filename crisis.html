Crisis help

If you are in danger right now, call 911. For emotional crisis or suicidal thoughts, call one of these lines. You do not need to know what to say first.

Call 1553Toll-free Call 0919-057-1553Smart/TNT Call 0917-899-8727Globe/TM Call 0966-351-4518Globe/TM
What to expect when you call

A trained person listens, asks how you are, and helps you with next steps.

Helping someone else

Stay with them, listen without judging, and help them call a hotline. If they are in immediate danger, call 911. More tips: Supporting a friend.

I am a student and I am afraid to tell anyone

That is a common fear. The DepEd helpline 33733 can connect callers in distress to NCMH specialists. You can also tell one adult you trust, like a teacher, guidance counselor, or relative.

What to expect when you call a crisis line

Takes the fear out of making the first call.

Text version (transcript)
  1. You do not need the right words.
  2. Dial 1553 (toll-free) or another number on the Crisis help page.
  3. A trained person answers and listens.
  4. Share as much or as little as you like.
  5. They ask how you are and help you plan a next step.
  6. In danger right now? Call 911.
