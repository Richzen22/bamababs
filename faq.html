Frequently asked questions

Is KalingaPH free to use?

Yes. There are no accounts, sign-ups, or fees.

Do you keep my searches?

No. Searches happen in your browser. See the privacy page.

Can KalingaPH diagnose me?

No. It only helps you find services and information.

What if a number does not connect?

Try another number for the same service on Crisis help, or call 911 if you are in danger.

Can I use it on a slow connection or a phone?

Yes. Pages are plain HTML and the layout adjusts to small screens. Videos are small files.

How do I change the text size or colors?

Use the “Text” and “Light / dark” buttons at the top of every page. See Accessibility.

Is the site available in Filipino?

Not yet. Some key phrases appear in Filipino. A full translation is planned.

How can I report a mistake or a broken link?

Contact the site author listed on the About page.
