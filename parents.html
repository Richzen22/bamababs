For parents and guardians

You do not need perfect words. Showing up, listening, and getting help early matter most.

Start the conversation

  • Choose a calm moment, like during a walk or a meal. Avoid doing it right after a conflict.
  • Try: “I have noticed you seem tired lately. I am here if you want to talk.”
  • Listen without judging or comparing. Thank them for telling you.

Take the next step together

  • Talk with the school adviser or guidance counselor.
  • Look for free or low-cost options in Find help.
  • Ask clinics about fees, schedule, and whether they offer online sessions.

Take care of yourself too

Supporting a child can be tiring. Short breaks and the coping skills on this site work for adults as well.

In danger right now? Call 911. For emotional crisis or suicidal thoughts, call 1553 (toll-free). See all crisis numbers.

KalingaPH shares general information. It does not diagnose or replace care from a licensed professional.
