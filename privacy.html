Privacy

Short version: we do not collect your data.

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking cookies.
  • Your searches stay in your browser.
  • Your theme and text size choice is saved only in your own browser, using local storage. Clear your browser data to remove it.
  • The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may share your IP address with Google when it does. The site still works with default fonts if they do not load.
  • Links to other sites follow those sites’ own privacy rules.

If you use a shared computer, close the tab and clear your history after visiting sensitive pages.
