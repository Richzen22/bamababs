Laws and school support

Two Philippine laws shape mental health support in schools. This is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

Mental Health Act (Republic Act 11036, 2018)

Signed in June 2018, this law sets a national mental health policy. It aims to make services more accessible and to protect the rights of people who use mental health services. It also covers mental health in schools and workplaces.

Basic Education Mental Health and Well-Being Promotion Act (Republic Act 12080, 2024)

Signed in December 2024, this law calls for school-based mental health programs in public and private basic education. News reports describe it as covering screenings, crisis response, referrals, and new school counselor positions. Its implementing rules were later signed by the Department of Education.

What this means for you

  • Students can ask their school about the mental health services available.
  • Teachers and staff are also covered by well-being programs.
  • Services still depend on local resources. If your school has none yet, use Find help.

Details of how each school applies these laws can differ. Ask your school or the Department of Education (opens in a new tab) for current guidance.
