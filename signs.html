Signs to look for

Everyone has hard days. These signs, especially when they last more than two weeks, are a reason to talk to someone.

Changes you may notice

  • Feelings: sadness, worry, irritability, or feeling empty or numb.
  • Body: trouble sleeping, sleeping too much, headaches or stomach aches with no clear cause, changes in appetite.
  • Behavior: pulling away from friends, skipping class, giving up things they once enjoyed.
  • Learning: trouble concentrating, falling grades, unfinished work.

Urgent signs: get help now

Talk about wanting to die or hurting oneself, giving away belongings, or saying goodbye. Do not leave the person alone. Call 911 or 1553.

Different cultures and families talk about distress in different ways. A person might say “pagod na pagod na ako” (I am so tired) when they mean more than sleepiness. Take what people tell you seriously.

What to do next

Read how to ask for help at school, how to support a friend, or find a counselor.
